About Cruise'N'Pass

About Cruise'N'Pass: a Manchester driving school built around the learner

Cruise'N'Pass is a local driving school teaching manual and automatic lessons across 30+ Greater Manchester neighbourhoods. Every instructor is on the DVSA Approved Driving Instructor (ADI) register, and every lesson is one-to-one in a dual-controlled car.

We started with a simple idea: lessons should be honest, structured and built around the test centre you will actually use. That means a clear plan, mock tests before your test is booked, and no pretending we can sell you a guaranteed pass.

What we believe about learning to drive

Passing the test is the minimum. Being safe after it is the point. Our instructors teach observation, planning and judgement alongside the manoeuvres — the skills that keep working when the test is over.

A plan, not a random hour

Every learner gets a structured progression from first controls to test standard, reviewed as you go.

Mock tests before the real thing

We only recommend booking your DVSA test when mock results show you are genuinely ready.

Honest hours

You get an estimate after your first lesson, not a sales pitch. If you need fewer hours, we will say so.

No route memorising

DVSA does not publish test routes. We teach the roads, junctions and road types local tests commonly use — not a script.

What you can expect from us

A proper first lesson

Your instructor assesses what you can already do, explains the plan and gives you an honest hours estimate — with no pressure to book a block.

Straight answers

If we do not think you are ready for your test, we will say so. If we cannot cover your area or preferred gearbox, we will tell you before you pay.

A reply within 24 hours

Call, WhatsApp or use the booking form. We aim to reply within 24 hours, seven days a week, 8am–8pm.

Where we teach

We cover Manchester city centre, Salford, Trafford, Stockport, Bolton, Bury and Rochdale, plus the neighbourhoods in between. Lessons run seven days a week, 8am–8pm, with free pick-up from home, work or university across the areas we cover.

We train around nine DVSA test centres: Cheetham Hill, West Didsbury, Sale, Bury, Chadderton, Bolton, Bredbury, Rochdale and Atherton. Your instructor will recommend the one whose roads you have practised most — not the one that is easiest for us to book.
