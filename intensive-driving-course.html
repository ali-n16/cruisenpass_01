Intensive Driving Courses in Manchester

An intensive course packs focused hours into a short time-frame so you can be test-ready quickly — ideal with a job start date, university term or a theory certificate expiring. Unlike weekly driving lessons in Manchester, intensives run concentrated sessions across consecutive days or weeks, all on your likely test routes.

Two fixed-price options: Fast Track Pass — 30hrs, £990 (£33/hr) and Zero to Hero — 40hrs, £1,320 (£33/hr) — both cheaper per hour than £35/hr pay-as-you-go (see full prices). Not sure you need 30+ hours? Read how many lessons you need first.

How it works

  1. Book via our form or call 07916 155054 to discuss availability — we can usually start within days.
  2. We plan your hours around your schedule — concentrated sessions across consecutive days or spaced over weeks, with free pick-up anywhere in Greater Manchester, 7am–8pm.
  3. Lessons focus on real test routes around Cheetham Hill, West Didsbury, Sale, Bury and Chadderton plus exam-style mock tests.
  4. Your instructor books the DVSA test only when mock results show a genuine chance to pass — preparation first, convenience second.

Is intensive right for you?

Good fit: deadlines, fast learners, part-trained drivers, confident learners who retain skills under pressure. Think twice: nervous beginners who need time between lessons, tight budgets (weekly blocks spread cost), or anyone expecting a guaranteed pass — no honest school guarantees that; DVSA waiting times also apply.
