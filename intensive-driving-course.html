Short FAQ

How fast can I start?

We can usually start intensive blocks within days depending on instructor availability and your schedule.

How many hours will I need?

Intensive learners commonly take 30–40 hours. Your instructor will give an honest estimate during planning.

Do you book the test for me?

Yes — your instructor can book the DVSA test when you are ready. We only book tests when the instructor is confident you have a good chance to pass.
