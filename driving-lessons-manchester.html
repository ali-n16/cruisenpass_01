City centre vs suburbs: where will you learn?

Manchester is two tests in one. Central lessons cover bus lanes, tram-shared junctions around St Peter's Square, box junctions and multi-lane roundabouts on the inner ring road — see our dedicated driving lessons in Manchester City Centre page. Suburban lessons in Moss Side, Longsight, Levenshulme and Didsbury build residential observation, parked-car meets and independent sat-nav driving. Your instructor blends both so test day holds no surprises, whether you test at Cheetham Hill or West Didsbury.
