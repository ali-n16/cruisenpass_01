Privacy Policy

Cruise'N'Pass keeps tracking to a minimum: our analytics are privacy-first and cookie-free, the contact forms are handled by a form processor, and the only cookies on this site are small advertising-measurement cookies described below. There is no consent banner on this website — if you prefer, you can block or delete these cookies at any time in your browser settings.

Plausible Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics to understand which pages of this website are visited and how visitors arrive (e.g. via Google, social media or direct links).

Plausible is privacy-first: it does not use cookies, does not collect personal data, does not track you across other sites, and does not record your IP address beyond what is required for security. No consent banner is required to comply with UK PECR / GDPR.

Data collected: page URL, referrer, browser, country (from IP, then discarded), device type. Aggregated only — no per-visitor profile is built.

Provider: Plausible Analytics, Dublin.

Google Ads conversion measurement

Our homepage uses Google Ads to measure how many people who click or view our adverts then phone us or submit a booking enquiry. For this purpose, Google may set advertising cookies on your device (for example _gcl_aw and _gcl_au) and a small amount of data about your visit (page URL, timestamp, whether a phone-call button or booking form was used) may be shared with Google.

This is a measurement-only setup: we do not use remarketing or audience profiles on this website at this time. Google handles this data under its own ads privacy policy, and you can opt out of ad measurement at adssettings.google.com or by removing cookies from your browser settings.

Embedded videos (TikTok & YouTube)

Some pages embed videos from TikTok and YouTube. When those embeds are displayed, content is loaded directly from TikTok or Google (YouTube is served in privacy-enhanced mode), and those providers may collect your IP address and other information in line with their own privacy policies. We do not control data collection by third-party video providers, so review their policies if you play embedded videos.

Contact form

If you submit the booking form, we receive your name, phone number, email, selected package and message. If you submit the instructor-application form, we receive your first name, last name, phone number, email, instructor status, areas covered and teaching experience. We use that information to respond to your enquiry and manage the relevant booking or recruitment conversation.

The booking form is submitted through Formspree, which acts as our form-processing provider. Formspree may process and retain submissions according to its privacy policy. The instructor application form is likewise submitted through Formspree; if the form cannot connect, the page falls back to opening your own email application so the details can be sent to our recruitment inbox (cruisenpass@gmail.com) directly. We do not sell your information.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to delete data you've submitted via our forms: cruisenpass@gmail.com.
