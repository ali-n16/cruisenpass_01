# Privacy Policy Cruise'N'Pass does not set marketing or tracking cookies. ## Plausible Analytics We use Plausible Analytics to understand which pages of this website are visited and how visitors arrive (e.g. via Google, social media or direct links). Plausible is privacy-first: it does not use cookies, does not collect personal data, does not track you across other sites, and does not record your IP address beyond what is required for security. No consent banner is required to comply with UK PECR / GDPR. Data collected: page URL, referrer, browser, country (from IP, then discarded), device type. Aggregated only — no per-visitor profile is built. Provider: [Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io/data-policy), Dublin. ## Contact form If you submit the booking or instructor-application form, we receive your name, phone number, email and the message you typed. We use that information only to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell or share it. ## Your rights You can ask us at any time to delete data you've submitted via our forms: cruisenpass@gmail.com.
