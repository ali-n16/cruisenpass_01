## Milestones (from the action plan §9.5) — check against reality

- **Weeks 1–4:** GBP fully optimised; +15–25 reviews; GSC impressions +20–40%; new pages indexed.
- **Months 2–3:** long-tails in positions 5–20; ~175 total reviews; geo-grid top-3 at 30–50% of points for area terms.
- **Months 4–6:** area cluster top-5; "driving lessons in Manchester" top 10–5 organic; ~350 reviews; leads 2–3× baseline.
- **Months 7–12:** head terms top 3 organic; Map Pack #1–3 across most of the grid in your radius; 500+ reviews.

**If a milestone slips, diagnose in this order:** (1) review velocity → (2) GBP activity gaps
(posts/photos/Q&A) → (3) area-page content budget → (4) referring domains → (5) on-page tweaks.
