## 5. Review system (100 → 500+)

**Get your review link (once):** GBP dashboard → "Ask for reviews" → copy the short
`g.page/.../review` link. Save it as a WhatsApp quick-reply and on every instructor's
phone homescreen. Print it as a QR card for the car.

**Day 0 — same day they pass (WhatsApp/SMS + verbal ask in the car):**
> Hi [Name] — what a result today at [test centre]! 🎉 If you have 60 seconds, a Google
> review really helps our small school get found by other learners in [area]: [g.page
> link]. Thank you — and congratulations again, [instructor] & the Cruise'N'Pass team.

**Day 3 — one follow-up if no review yet (final ask — no third message, ever):**
> Hi [Name] — hope you're enjoying the independence! If you've got a moment, honest
> feedback on Google helps other [area] learners find us: [g.page link]. Thanks so much 🙏

**Verbal script (instructor, pass day, before they drive away):**
> "You've made my week — genuinely. If you could drop us a Google review, it makes a
> huge difference to a small school like ours. I'll send you the link now."

**QR card copy (leave in the car):**
> Passed? Please review us on Google — 30 seconds, and it genuinely helps other
> Manchester learners find a good instructor. [QR code → g.page link]

**Policy line (never cross these):**
- ✅ Ask **every** pupil — gating (asking only happy customers) breaches Google policy and CMA rules.
- ❌ No incentives of any kind (money, discounts, free lessons, prize draws) in exchange for reviews.
- ❌ No reviews from your own devices/premises/IP; no bought reviews; no "happy customers → Google, unhappy → us" flows.
- ✅ Reply to every review within 72h, naturally including area + service:
  *"Thanks Amalu — really glad the Chadderton test-route practice paid off. Safe driving!"*

**Target pace:** +15–25 in month 1 → +25–35/month → +35–45/month. Reviews mentioning
instructor name + test centre + area are gold (visible in AI answers too).
