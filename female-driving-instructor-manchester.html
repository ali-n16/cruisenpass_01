Why Some Learners Prefer a Female Instructor

Learning to drive is personal. Common reasons learners give us: feeling more comfortable asking "silly" questions (there are none), preferring a calmer teaching style, cultural or religious preferences around who they spend lessons with, or simply having learned better with women teachers in the past. All of them are good enough reasons. Our job is to make the car a place where you can concentrate on driving — nothing else.

What Stays Exactly the Same

Everything about the standard. Every Cruise'N'Pass instructor is a qualified Approved Driving Instructor on the DVSA register, teaching one-to-one in a dual-controlled car, following the same structured syllabus — from first clutch control (or your first automatic lesson) through roundabouts, dual carriageways, independent driving and every manoeuvre, to mock tests under exam conditions around your local test centre. Same prices, too: no premium for requesting a female instructor.

How to Request One

Call 07916 155054, message us on WhatsApp, or use the booking form — just say you'd prefer a female instructor and tell us your area and whether you want manual or automatic. We'll confirm availability before you book or pay for anything, and we'll arrange for you to speak with your instructor before your first lesson. If it turns out not to be the right fit after you start, you can change instructor at any time — no awkwardness, no fee.
