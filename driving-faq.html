Driving Lesson FAQs

Answers to common questions about lessons, booking and what to expect. If you can't find what you need, call 07916 155054.

How much are driving lessons in Manchester?

Cruise'N'Pass offers pay-as-you-go lessons at £35 per hour (2-hour minimum) and discounted block packages starting from £320 for 10 hours. All pricing is transparent with no hidden fees.

What areas in Greater Manchester do you cover?

We currently provide driving lessons in Atherton, Bolton, Bury, Bredbury, Chadderton, Cheetham Hill, West Didsbury, and across Greater Manchester.

Do you offer intensive driving courses?

Yes, our Fast Track Pass (30 hours, £990) and Zero to Hero (40 hours, £1,320) packages are designed for learners who want to pass their test quickly and comprehensively.

How do I book a driving lesson?

You can book a lesson by calling 07916 155054, emailing cruisenpass@gmail.com, or using the booking form on our website. We offer flexible scheduling 7 days a week from 7am to 8pm.

How many lessons will I need?

Most learners need between 20 and 40 hours. Your instructor will give you an honest estimate after your first lesson.

Can I drive on the motorway as a learner?

Yes — since 2018 learners may drive on motorways with an approved instructor in a dual-control car.

Do you help with the theory test?

Our instructors support theory preparation and can point you to practice materials; we also offer free tutorials on the Learn Now page.

What if I need to reschedule?

Contact us by phone or email. We offer flexible scheduling 7 days a week from 7am to 8pm and will work with you to rearrange lessons where possible.

Still have questions?

Call us on 07916 155054 or use the booking form on the homepage.
