Driving Test Centres in Greater Manchester

We regularly use a number of DVSA test centres across Greater Manchester. Below is a short guide to the centres we commonly book learners into and the types of roads and conditions you can expect in each area.

Choosing a test centre

Instructors usually recommend the test centre whose routes you have practised most with. We consider journey time, the kinds of junctions and roads on the test routes, and learner confidence when suggesting the best centre.

Test centres we commonly use

  • Chadderton — sits between Oldham and Manchester with the A627(M) and Broadway corridors close by, so learners get realistic test-route practice on busy dual carriageway approaches and residential junctions.
  • Cheetham Hill (Aldergrove Road) — Cheetham Hill Road and nearby streets are test-route staples; expect urban roundabouts, bus lanes and tram-aware junctions that sharpen observation and lane discipline.
  • West Didsbury — one of the busier test centres, West Didsbury's routes include village-style streets and nearby A-roads so lessons here replicate probable test conditions closely.
  • Bury — lessons around Bury mix market-town traffic with the A58 and Metrolink crossings, helping learners practise the junctions and roundabouts commonly found on local tests.
  • Bolton — Bolton offers a mix of town-centre traffic and quieter residential streets; local routes include busy A-class approaches and challenging town-centre junctions.
  • Sale — with the Sale test centre nearby, learners practise crossroads, park-adjacent manoeuvres and the A56 approaches that feature on many local tests.
  • Stockport — famous local features such as the large roundabout systems and A6 corridors make Stockport a good proving ground for lane discipline and anticipation.
  • Bredbury — lessons here include busy interchange junctions and the A560 corridor, preparing learners for test routes that demand confident lane control.
  • Rochdale — a mix of A-road corridors and town-centre one-way systems means learners practise both higher-speed decisions and close-quarters city driving.
  • Atherton — Atherton's A577 and A579 corridors plus quiet residential streets make it an area where learners can practise both rural-style driving and busier commuter routes.

Note: test-centre characteristics can change and pass rates vary — check the latest official pass rates and test-centre information on GOV.UK before booking.

How we pick the right centre for you

We choose a test centre based on the routes you have practised, your confidence on the likely roads and the practicalities of travel time. Your instructor will only recommend a booking when they believe you are ready to take the test — preparation first, convenience second.
