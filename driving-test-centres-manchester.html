Driving Test Centres in Greater Manchester

Booking driving lessons in Manchester? Where you test matters almost as much as how you train. We use DVSA test centres across Greater Manchester and train you on the roads and junctions local tests commonly use — roundabouts, bus lanes, dual carriageways and residential manoeuvres. We do not claim to know your exact route: DVSA does not publish test routes.

Below is our instructor-written guide to the centres we commonly book — what each area throws at you, who it suits, and which lesson package gets you test-ready. For the full learning path see how many lessons you need and our intensive courses.

Choosing a test centre

Instructors usually recommend the test centre whose roads you have practised most. We consider journey time, the kinds of junctions and roads on local tests, and learner confidence when suggesting the best centre. Rule of thumb: pass where you practise — we only book when mock tests show you are ready.

Test centres we commonly use

Cheetham Hill

Urban roundabouts, bus lanes and tram-aware junctions on the Cheetham Hill Road corridor. Best for confident city drivers in Manchester, Crumpsall and Broughton.

Full Cheetham Hill guide

West Didsbury

One of the busiest centres — village streets plus A-roads. Ideal if you train in Didsbury, Withington, Chorlton or Burnage.

Sale

A56 crossroads, Sale Water Park roads and residential grids. Great for Sale, Stretford and Old Trafford learners.

Bury

Market-town traffic, A58 stretches and Metrolink crossings. Suits Bury learners who want clear roundabout and junction practice.

Chadderton

A627(M) and Broadway corridors — dual-carriageway approaches plus residential junctions. See Chadderton lessons.

Bolton

Town-centre traffic around the A666 plus quieter routes in Burnden and Harwood.

Bredbury

The DVSA centre serving Stockport, Cheadle and Hazel Grove. Expect large roundabout systems, the A6 corridor and the A560 interchange — a proper lane-discipline test.

Rochdale

A-road corridors plus town-centre one-ways. Instructor Hassan Iqbal guided a zero-minor pass here; see Rochdale lessons.

Atherton

The local centre for Atherton learners, with busy arterial roads and quieter streets towards Hag Fold. Bolton is a short drive away if it suits your practice better.

Note: test-centre characteristics can change. DVSA does not publish official test routes, so we teach the area and road types rather than a fixed route.

Test-centre FAQs

Which Manchester test centre has the easiest routes?

There is no official ranking of easiest routes. Pass rates vary by learner population, route mix and traffic, and routes change. We recommend the centre whose roads you have practised most — usually Cheetham Hill, West Didsbury, Sale, Bury or Chadderton for Manchester learners.

Which test centre has the shortest wait?

DVSA's September 2026 data shows the shortest median waits at Rochdale (5.6 weeks) and Bury (5.7), and the longest at West Didsbury (19.9) and Sale (18.3). Waiting times change monthly, so check the current table above before choosing.

Is there a Stockport test centre?

No — Bredbury (Manchester) is the DVSA centre serving Stockport. There is no separate Stockport test centre, so plan around Bredbury if you live in Stockport, Cheadle or Hazel Grove.

How do I choose between Cheetham Hill and West Didsbury?

Cheetham Hill suits confident urban drivers (bus lanes, tram junctions, dense traffic). West Didsbury suits learners who want village streets plus A-road stretches. Your instructor advises after seeing you drive both types of roads.

Do you teach around my test centre before booking?

Yes — we train on the roads and junctions local tests commonly use, run mock tests under exam conditions, and only book when mock results show you are ready.

How many lessons before a Manchester test?

Most learners need 20–40 hours. See our guide on how many driving lessons you need and our Manchester lesson prices from £35/hr.
